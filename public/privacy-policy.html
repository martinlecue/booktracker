Privacy Policy

BookTracker · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how BookTracker (“the App”), published by Martin Le Cué, collects, uses and protects your personal information.

In short: we only collect what the App needs to work. We never sell your data, and there are no ads in the App.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and display name, provided when you sign up through Firebase Authentication (email or Sign in with Apple).
  • Library data: the books you add, reading statuses, notes, ratings, reviews and progress.
  • Reading sessions: pages read, duration and date of each session.
  • Goals, shelves and achievements you create or earn in the App.
  • Social features (optional): if you choose a username and add reading buddies, your username and your reading activity (book started, pages read, book finished) are visible to the friends you connect with.
  • Creator code (optional): if you enter a creator's code, it is attached to your purchases so that creator can be thanked.

We do not collect your location, contacts or photos. The camera is only used, on your device, to scan a book's barcode.

How we use your information

  • Sync your library across your devices
  • Show your statistics, streaks and reading progress
  • Let you sign in and access your account
  • Provide the optional social features you turn on

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google): authentication and data storage. Firebase privacy
  • RevenueCat: in-app purchase and subscription management. RevenueCat privacy
  • Google Books API and Open Library: book search, covers and details. Only your search terms are sent. Google privacy · Open Library terms

Some book pages include a link to buy the book on Amazon. These are affiliate links: if you tap one, Amazon may know you came from BookTracker, under Amazon's own privacy policy. No personal data is sent by the App.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on Firebase servers (Google Cloud). We use standard security measures to protect it against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

Data is kept as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from your Profile in the App (“Delete my account”); your online data is then erased.

In-app purchases

BookTracker offers in-app purchases (monthly subscription and lifetime access). Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store and managed with RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete and export your personal data (CSV export is available in the App). To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Children

BookTracker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has given us data, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
