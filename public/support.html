Frequently asked questions

Is BookTracker free?

Yes. You can download it for free and track up to 10 books. BookTracker Premium unlocks an unlimited library and extras like shelves, reviews and ambient sounds, as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I import my books from Goodreads or StoryGraph?

First export your library as a CSV file. On Goodreads: My Books → Import and export → Export Library. On StoryGraph: Manage Account → Export StoryGraph Library. Then, in BookTracker, open Discover, tap “Import list” and choose the file. Your ratings, reading dates and statuses come along.

I bought Premium but it isn't active.

Open the Premium screen and tap “Restore purchases”, using the same Apple Account you paid with. If it still doesn't work, email me with the date of the purchase.

Does BookTracker work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. Sign in with the same account and your library syncs between your devices.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open your Profile and tap “Delete my account”. Your online data is erased. For security reasons, the app may ask you to sign in again first.

Which languages are available?

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese and Dutch. BookTracker follows your iPhone's language.
