Inside the app

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Scan, search or import

Scan an ISBN barcode, search by title or author, or bring your whole Goodreads or StoryGraph library in one go.

Reading timer

A calm timer with ambient sounds (rain, forest, fireplace) that logs your pages and minutes for you.

Streaks, XP and levels

Every page earns XP. Keep your daily streak alive and level up as you read.

Stats and goals

Books, pages, reading pace and a yearly goal, all in clear, beautiful charts.

Your year in books

A story-style recap of your reading year, ready to share with your friends.

Widgets, reminders, sync

Home Screen widgets, a gentle daily reminder and your library synced across iPhone and iPad.
